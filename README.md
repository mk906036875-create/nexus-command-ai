## 🧠 What is NEXUS COMMAND AI?

**NEXUS COMMAND AI** is an interactive enterprise decision-intelligence prototype designed to help business leaders understand:

- Revenue exposure
- Customer risk
- Conversion leakage
- Cancellation risk
- Fulfillment pressure
- Response-time leakage
- Recovery opportunities
- Recommended next actions

NEXUS transforms business signals into an **executive-level decision view**.

## ⚡ Key Features

### 🔴 Revenue Risk Intelligence
Monitor multiple business signals and visualize potential revenue exposure.

### 🟢 Recovery Opportunity
Estimate where intervention may create the highest recovery opportunity.

### ⚡ AI Decision Engine
NEXUS prioritizes the next strategic action based on current signal conditions.

### 📡 Live Signal Simulation
Adjust business signals and instantly see how the intelligence layer responds.

### 🔮 What-If Simulator
Test scenarios such as:

- Higher conversion
- Lower cancellation
- Better fulfillment
- Faster customer response

### 🤖 AI Analyst
Ask NEXUS questions such as:

> “Where should I focus first?”

> “What is creating the highest revenue risk?”

> “Which action should I execute next?”
